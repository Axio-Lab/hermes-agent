# Exporting Content for the Audit

Not every platform makes exporting content easy — screenshots are always a valid fallback. Where export tools exist:

## Meta (Facebook / Instagram) via Meta Business Suite

1. Go to the **Content** sidebar and click **Export data**.
2. Choose Facebook or Instagram, select the pages, date range, and data view/content level/filter settings, then click **Generate**.

## Other platforms

For LinkedIn, X, TikTok, YouTube, etc., check their current analytics/insights section for an export or download option. These menus change over time — if a path is outdated, screenshots of content and engagement numbers work just as well as raw exports for this system.

# How to summarize X/Twitter posts

You're pulling out the posts from curated industry leaders that are actually worth
someone's attention this period, out of everything they posted.

Rules:

- Only include posts that state an opinion, share a firsthand data point, or make a
  prediction — skip replies, retweets without added commentary, memes, and pure
  self-promotion.
- Group by person, not by post. One line of context on why this person's take
  matters here is fine only if it's not already obvious from the curated list's
  `why` field.
- Rewrite the substance in your own words rather than reproducing the post text
  verbatim; paraphrase is the default, any direct quote must stay under 15 words.
- If two leaders disagree publicly on the same topic this period, surface that
  tension explicitly — it's more useful than either take alone.
- Cap it at the 5-8 most substantive items across the whole industry for this
  digest, not 5-8 per person.
- Always link to the original post.

# How to summarize LinkedIn activity

Pull posts, articles, and comments from curated operator profiles that are worth
attention this period.

Rules:

- Prefer original posts, long-form articles, and firsthand lessons from building or
  running something. Skip generic motivational quotes, hiring spam without insight,
  and pure reshares with no added take.
- Group by person. One line of context on why they matter in this industry is fine
  if not obvious from the curated `why` field.
- Paraphrase by default. Direct quotes stay under 15 words.
- Surface disagreements between two tracked leaders on the same topic when both
  posted this period.
- Cap at 5-8 substantive items across all LinkedIn sources for this digest.
- Always link to the original post or article URL.

# How to summarize Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook updates

Use this for Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook pages listed in the industry file.
These platforms skew toward influencers; apply a higher bar than X or LinkedIn.

Rules:

- Include only updates with a concrete operating lesson, product launch detail,
  metric, process change, or original industry take. Skip lifestyle, memes, and
  engagement bait.
- Note the platform in each bullet (Instagram / TikTok / Facebook).
- For video platforms, summarize the substance; link to the post or reel.
- Cap at 3-5 items total across all social platforms unless the user asked for more.
- If a profile had nothing substantive this period, omit it rather than padding.

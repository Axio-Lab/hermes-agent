# How to summarize a podcast appearance

You're summarizing a podcast episode or interview featuring one of the curated
industry leaders, for someone busy who wants the substance without watching or
listening to the whole thing.

Rules:

- 3-5 bullet points max per episode.
- Lead with the single most concrete or contrarian claim made, not the guest's bio
  or the show's usual intro chat.
- Prefer specific numbers, named mechanisms, or named tactics over vague sentiment
  ("raised prices 12% and churn didn't move" beats "discussed pricing strategy").
- If the guest disagreed with conventional wisdom in the industry, say what the
  conventional wisdom was and what they said instead.
- Skip sponsor reads, banter, and generic career-advice filler.
- End with one line noting who else in the episode is worth knowing (co-host,
  other guest) only if genuinely relevant.
- Never fabricate a quote. If you're not confident in exact wording, paraphrase
  instead of quoting, and keep any direct quote under 15 words.
- Always include the episode title, guest name, and link.

# Writing evaluation

Run this after editing. Treat every item as pass or fail. Fix failures before
returning the draft.

## Fidelity

1. Does the edit preserve the writer's point without adding claims, examples,
   statistics, quotations, sources, or opinions?
2. Does it preserve recognizable vocabulary, cadence, bluntness, humor,
   uncertainty, digressions, and level of polish?
3. Did strong human sentences survive instead of being rewritten merely for
   consistency?
4. Is the amount of cutting proportional to the actual problem?
5. Does the writer still sound like themselves?

## Clarity

1. Does the draft lead with what the reader needs while keeping setup that
   adds context, tension, or character?
2. Are tangled sentences clearer without flattening natural spoken rhythm?
3. Do concrete facts and useful details survive?
4. Do direct verbs and named actors replace empty abstractions where possible?
5. Was structure preserved unless changing it materially improved the piece?

## Slop patterns

1. Are throat-clearing and faux-insight setups gone?
2. Are unnecessary binary contrasts, negative lists, colon reveals,
   rhetorical setups, and dramatic fragments gone?
3. Did superficial analysis become a real consequence or mechanism?
4. Did importance puffery become a plain fact?
5. Are vague attributions supported by named sources or flagged?
6. Are fake-strong verbs and synonym cycling fixed?
7. Is the rhythm free of mechanical symmetry and forced groups of three?
8. Are fake-profound kickers and redundant recap endings gone?
9. Does formatting serve the content rather than decorate it?
10. Are em dashes, filler phrases, inflated vocabulary, and decorative emoji
    absent unless the user explicitly requested them?

## Delivery

1. Does the full edited draft appear in the response?
2. Is `What changed` short, specific, and honest about major restructuring?
3. Would the draft sound natural when read to a sharp colleague?

For detection-only requests, verify that every finding names a pattern, quotes
evidence, and suggests a short fix without rewriting, scoring, or guessing AI
authorship.
